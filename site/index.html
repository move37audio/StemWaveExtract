Your files stay yours

Runtime and models live inside the app. Preferences, caches, and earlier-version data may live in ~/Library/Application Support/com.stemwave.extract. Exported audio stays in your chosen save folder. To uninstall, quit and delete the app and that Application Support folder. Your exported stems remain unless you delete them separately.
